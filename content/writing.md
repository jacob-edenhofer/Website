I write essays, research summaries, and [Twitter threads](https://threadreaderapp.com/user/edenhofer_jacob) on climate policy, comparative political economy, and liberal democracy. A recurring theme is how mainstream parties respond to the radical right. I discuss the [accommodation debate](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1863743988974416222.html) on immigration and the arguments for, and difficulties of, maintaining a [*Brandmauer* or cordon sanitaire](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1993758805000147137.html) vis-à-vis radical-right populist parties, like the AfD. Other themes include the normalisation of radical positions, voters’ responses to violations of democratic norms, the political consequences of economic insecurity, and the political economy of policy advice. I also try to synthesise classic bodies of work in comparative political economy, including [redistributive preferences](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1825935220031500339.html), [veto players](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1736338887486640624.html), [long-term policymaking](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1605515288107024385.html), and patterns of democracy (e.g. [Lijphart](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1690469312090263553.html) and [Ganghof](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1878379229114487135.html)). I started these threads when I was revising for my “Comparative Government” exams as a way to get feedback on my revision notes. This page also collects my public discussions and presentations.
