I write essays, research summaries, and [Twitter threads](https://threadreaderapp.com/user/edenhofer_jacob) on climate policy, comparative political economy, and liberal democracy. A recurring theme is how mainstream parties respond to the radical right. I discuss the [accommodation debate](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1863743988974416222.html) on immigration and the arguments for, and difficulties of, maintaining a [Brandmauer or cordon sanitaire](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1993758805000147137.html) vis-à-vis radical-right populist parties, like the AfD. Other themes include the normalisation of radical positions, voters’ responses to violations of democratic norms, the political consequences of economic insecurity, and expertise as a source of information and political legitimation. I try to synthesise the literature and offer tentative interpretations of its implications. This page also collects my public discussions and presentations.
